Decisions taken in the wave:
- Design § 11 D20 grew with each adapter: a ratio whose numerator matches nothing over a non-empty
  population reads unknown in every series, in Prometheus, Loki, SQL and Git alike.
- Design § 11 D21: the HTTP probe reads down only for a `not_found` or `timeout` refusal of its own
  invoke, and invokes only a catalog read whose description names profile `generic-http`.
- The `thalamus.definition.Unit` alphabet is ASCII 33–126; design § 8 states that neither generated
  decoder enforces it, and `parse_ucum` does (bounded at 64 nesting levels).
- Conformance ids come from a process-wide sequence, zero-padded so store order is issue order; the
  Eventlog store mints ULIDs.
- `git.count` with no match reads 0: a quiet window can count zero.

Open outside this repository: two ESS generator gaps (openapi `x-ess-attributes` numbers published
as `serde_json` wrappers; the `Unit` alphabet not enforced by either decoder), and two Connectors gaps
(probe latency; telling a provider 5xx from Connectors' own outage).
